Our Story

Zazu's Coffee was founded in 2018 by Amara Odhiambo, a Nairobi-born barista who spent five years honing her craft in specialty coffee shops across Europe before returning home with one goal: to create a world-class coffee experience rooted in Kenyan heritage.

What started as a single 12-seater café in Westlands has grown into four branches across Nairobi. Every cup we serve traces its journey back to the highlands of Nyeri and Kirinyaga — farms we visit personally, farmers we know by name.

What We Stand For

Three values that guide everything we do.

Quality First

We never compromise on our beans, our roasts or our brewing methods. Every cup must meet the Zazu's standard before it reaches your hands.

Community Roots

We reinvest in the farming communities that make our coffee possible — through fair pay, training programmes and long-term partnerships.

Sustainable Futures

From biodegradable packaging to solar-powered roasting, we are committed to leaving a lighter footprint on Nairobi and Kenya.
