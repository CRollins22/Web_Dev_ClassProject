Why Zazu's?

More than just coffee — a Nairobi experience.

Locally Sourced

We work directly with small-scale farmers across Nyeri, Kirinyaga and Murang'a to bring you the freshest Kenyan beans.

Expert Roasting

Our in-house roasters craft each batch with precision — light, medium or dark, every roast is intentional and flavour-forward.

Nairobi's Own

Born in Westlands and now across the city, Zazu's Coffee is proudly Nairobi-grown and deeply community-focused.
